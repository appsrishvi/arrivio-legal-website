Your privacy matters. This policy explains the information Arrivio needs to power its location-based arrival reminders and the practical choices you have over that information.

At a glance

No sign-up. Local alarm storage. Transparent map requests.

Arrivio does not require account creation. Saved destinations and arrival history remain on your device, while location searches and routes can involve third-party map providers.

01 Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Arrivio mobile application handles information. Arrivio is presented by Rishvi Ltd (referred to as “Arrivio”, “we”, “our” or “us” in this policy). Contact information is provided in Section 14.

This notice applies to the Arrivio mobile app and these Arrivio legal pages. It does not replace the separate privacy notices of map providers or of Rishvi’s wider corporate website.

Arrivio uses only the information needed to offer destination selection and arrival reminders. Depending on the features you use, this includes:

There is no mandatory account registration or sign-in in the user-facing app. We do not ask for your name, email address, password or payment details to set a destination alarm. If you contact us separately, we will use the contact details you provide to respond to your enquiry.

03 On-device storage and cloud accounts

Saved destinations, alarm configuration, arrival history and app preferences are stored using local device storage. Arrivio does not operate a user-profile database or a cloud account service for these records in the version covered by this policy. Your saved places and history are not automatically uploaded to an Arrivio-owned user database.

Device operating systems can provide their own backups or restoration features. If such a feature is enabled, any copying of local app data is governed by your device and backup-provider settings, not an Arrivio account.

Important distinction: local app storage does not mean every function is offline. Opening maps, searching addresses or requesting routes can send data to third-party services, as explained below.

04 External maps, search and routing providers

Arrivio uses Geoapify for map tiles, place lookup and route information when configured. It can also use services based on OpenStreetMap, including OpenStreetMap map tiles, Nominatim geocoding and the OSRM public routing service as fallbacks.

Requests may include the name or location you search for, destination coordinates, your starting coordinates or routing waypoints, and standard network data such as your IP address and request time. These services receive information needed to respond to the request. Their own data practices and retention periods may differ from ours.

  • Geoapify Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy
  • OSRM project information

Arrivio does not control third-party network logs or their independent retention decisions. Availability, accuracy and geographic coverage of maps and routes can change.

05 Permissions and background location

Arrivio requests device permissions only to support the functions you choose to use:

  • Location while using the app: to show your current position, select a destination and calculate distance.
  • Background/“Always allow” location: to monitor your approach and issue an arrival alert while the app is in the background or your phone is locked, subject to your operating system settings.
  • Notifications, alarm sound and vibration: to display an active tracking notification and alert you when your alarm conditions are met.
  • Device restart and foreground service features (where supported): to support active arrival reminders and system-managed recovery after a restart or app update.

You can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. With some permissions disabled, especially precise/background location or notifications, Arrivio may not be able to deliver a dependable arrival alarm. Giving an operating-system permission is not, by itself, consent for unrelated data uses.

06 Why information is used and our lawful basis

We use the information described above to provide the app functions you request: identifying destinations, displaying maps and routes, calculating proximity and generating arrival alerts. We do not use saved destination history for advertising or behavioural profiling in the version covered by this policy.

Where the UK GDPR applies to personal information for which Rishvi Ltd determines the purposes and means of processing, our legal basis is generally performance of a contract when handling information necessary to provide the map, routing and alarm features you request, and, where appropriate, our legitimate interests in maintaining the reliability and security of those services. Any legitimate-interest use is assessed against your rights and reasonable expectations.

Separate external providers may determine their own lawful bases for processing requests sent to them; refer to their linked policies for details. No data is requested because of a statutory obligation, and the app does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

07 Retention and deletion

Arrivio keeps your saved places and preferences in device storage until you change, remove or clear them. Arrival-event history can be cleared from the app and is limited to a rolling local history in the reviewed implementation. Disabling or removing an alarm removes its active configuration as the app permits.

  • Delete individual destinations in the app's saved-places feature.
  • Clear arrival history using the app's history controls.
  • Remove the app and, where available, clear its application data in device settings to erase remaining local records. Device backups, if enabled, may have separate retention rules.
  • Manage location/notification permissions through your phone's settings.

Because we do not keep an Arrivio cloud copy of your saved places, we normally cannot remotely retrieve or erase those local records for you. Third-party map providers set their own retention periods for their API requests; consult their privacy notices.

08 Security

Arrivio relies on the mobile operating system’s application isolation and access controls for locally saved settings and alarm records. Map and route services are contacted over HTTPS in the reviewed implementation. We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping the app and operating system updated.

No device, network service or transmission method is completely secure. Locally stored app preferences are not presented as end-to-end encrypted storage, so avoid entering sensitive personal information as custom location names.

09 International processing

Mapping providers and supporting network services may process requests in countries other than your own. For example, Geoapify describes European hosting for its APIs, while other providers may use distributed network infrastructure. Their privacy notices explain their practices.

Where Rishvi Ltd is responsible for any international transfer of personal data, we will comply with applicable data-protection requirements, including the use of appropriate safeguards where required.

10 Your rights and choices

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Where consent is the lawful basis for any processing, it can be withdrawn. You can make an enquiry using the contact details below.

Your right to object: where we rely on legitimate interests, you may object to processing based on that ground. We will assess such objections in accordance with applicable law.

Most personal information used by Arrivio remains only on your device, so the most direct way to review, change or delete it is through the app or your phone settings. For data held independently by map providers, you may need to contact those providers directly.

If you are in the United Kingdom and remain concerned about how personal information is handled, you may raise a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). We encourage you to contact us first so we can address the concern.

11 Children’s privacy

Arrivio is a general-purpose travel reminder tool, not a service designed specifically for children. We do not intentionally request children’s names or contact details to use the app. If you believe we have received information about a child through a support enquiry, please contact us so that we can review and handle it appropriately.

12 Privacy of these legal web pages

These standalone legal pages contain no registration form, advertising script, analytics tag or tracking cookies. The static HTML files do not set cookies themselves. However, the service hosting them may record ordinary technical web-server information such as IP addresses, browser details and request timestamps for security and delivery.

If these pages are embedded into a larger website or hosted with additional scripts, features or analytics, that website’s relevant notices and cookie controls may also apply.

13 Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when Arrivio’s features, providers or legal obligations change. We will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of the page. Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent for a significant change, we will provide it.
