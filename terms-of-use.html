02 About Arrivio

Arrivio is a location-based arrival reminder application. It lets you find or choose a destination, configure a proximity radius, activate a reminder and receive an alert when the device detects that you are approaching the selected location.

Depending on device support and permissions, it may show maps, estimated routes, saved locations, active alarm details and a local arrival history. Arrivio currently does not require account registration to use these features.

Routes, distances, estimates and map descriptions are provided for convenience. They are not turn-by-turn navigation instructions or a commitment that your chosen route is safe or accessible.

03 Permissions and availability

The core arrival-alert experience depends on permissions and device capabilities. Some features need precise location, background location, notifications, sound/vibration access and support for background services. We may request access through standard operating-system permission prompts.

You may decline or revoke permissions, but this can prevent an alarm from working correctly. Alerts may also be delayed, silenced or stopped by battery optimisation, power-saving modes, GPS signal conditions, device restart behaviour, software restrictions or app termination.

Map tiles, address lookups and routes can require an internet connection. Availability depends on external service providers, network access and your device configuration.

04 Travel safety and important limitations

Arrivio is a convenience reminder, not a safety-critical or emergency service. It cannot guarantee an alert at an exact distance or time. Location accuracy can be affected by tunnels, dense buildings, poor satellite reception, network restrictions and other conditions.

  • Do not rely on Arrivio as your sole reminder for a destination, transport connection, medical visit or other important commitment.
  • When driving, do not operate, read or configure the app in a way that distracts you or violates traffic laws.
  • Follow road signs, transport staff instructions and local conditions over any map, arrival estimate or automated alert.
  • Do not rely on Arrivio for emergencies, rescue, personal safety monitoring or services requiring guaranteed response times.

Always plan an independent backup for important journeys and keep your attention on your surroundings.
